Module 3: Second-Order Response

Mass-spring-damper (m x″ + b x′ + k x = F) driven by a step force F = k, so the final position is always 1 m. Move m, b and k and watch the response, the poles and the state.

Controls








Derived Parameters

Characteristics

Rise time: 10 % to 90 % of the final value. Settling time: last time outside a ±5 % band. Both are read off the curve.

State-Space Form

States: x₁ = x (position), x₂ = x′ (velocity). Model: x′ = A x + B F, output y = x₁.
